WHY THIS ONE
Most planners give you a checklist. This one is written by someone who has thought about where these weekends actually go wrong: money nobody agreed on, an itinerary with no gaps in it, a guest who cannot eat anything on the table, and no plan for getting home at 1am. There is a page for each of those.

It also includes the games, so you are not buying a planner and a games pack separately.

PERFECT FOR
The maid of honor, the bridesmaids, or whoever is organizing. Works for a destination weekend, a rented house, or a night out in your own city. Also works for hen parties, bridal weekends, and girls trips.
