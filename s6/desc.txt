Large Print Activities for Seniors. 10 printable pages set in 17pt type, with fewer items per page, for older adults who find standard print hard to read. Instant download.

Most activity sheets for seniors are printed at the same size as everything else, which leaves out the people most likely to need them. This pack is set in 17pt, single column, with generous line spacing and only six questions on a page, so nothing feels crowded and a line is easy to track.

- General Knowledge, Gently Asked, 12 questions across two easy pages
- Leader answer key for the questions
- This or That, 14 simple preference pairs that open into stories

- A note on reading and sight, for setting up the room

WHY 17PT MATTERS
Recognized large print guidance starts at 16 to 18pt. A lot of material sold as large print is only slightly bigger than normal. The reading text here is 17pt, the list text is 16pt, and the cut-out cards are 17pt, so the claim is something you can measure rather than take on trust.

PERFECT FOR
Activity directors, caregivers, and families working with older adults, including anyone with low vision, macular degeneration, or cataracts. Also useful in memory care, where less on a page means less to feel confused by.

HOW TO GET THE BEST RESULT
Print at full size with no scaling, on plain rather than glossy paper to reduce glare. Light from the side beats light from behind. Read each line aloud as well as showing it, so the activity works whether or not the person reads it themselves.
